The photographic treatment below remains a brief for a future video variant.

Setting and continuity
A peaceful Haryana village in warm early-morning light, with brick homes,
neem trees, and dairy courtyards. An adult Indian milkman wears an off-white
kurta-pajama and a simple turban. His black roadster bicycle carries a clean,
large stainless-steel milk collection can/drum securely on the rear carrier.
Keep the milkman, bicycle, clothing, and steel drum consistent across shots.

Shot 1 — first collection, approximately 0–5 seconds
The milkman arrives by bicycle at a village courtyard and stops. With the
bicycle stationary, a farmer pours fresh milk from a smaller steel vessel
into the milkman's open collection drum. Show the milk pouring clearly.

Shot 2 — second collection, approximately 5–10 seconds
The same milkman cycles a short distance to another home. He parks and
steadies the bicycle. Another farmer pours milk into the same steel drum.
Use a closer view of the hands, milk stream, and clean steel vessels.

Shot 3 — motorcycle arrival, approximately 10–15 seconds
At the second collection point, another adult milk collector arrives on a
motorcycle with steel milk cans secured to its sides. He parks, switches off
the engine, and joins the collection. End on both collectors beside the
milk cans in the village courtyard.

Visual direction
Natural movements, realistic hands and liquid, coherent vehicles, clean
milk handling, gentle camera movement, and consistent morning light.
No text, subtitles, watermarks, invented logos, or product packaging.
Avoid pouring while riding, distorted wheels, extra limbs, and abrupt
changes in the collectors' appearance. Quiet village ambience if supported.

Integration after a usable clip has been generated and checked
Save the actual playable MP4 under public/videos/ and set videoSrc on the
village reel to its /videos/... URL. The existing card already supports a
native video player with controls, inline playback, and a poster image.
Do not set videoSrc to a nonexistent file or label the brief as a video.
